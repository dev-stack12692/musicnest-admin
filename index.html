Only the MusicNest admin Google account can manage playlists. Playlists you publish appear in every user's app.

How it works

1. Click + New playlist, give it a title, add songs with the search box (or paste an exact YouTube link).
2. The emoji is the playlist's icon in the app when it has no cover image.
3. Tick Published and Save - within seconds the playlist appears on every user's Home screen and in search.
4. Share: in the app, open the playlist and tap the share button (top right) - friends get the playlist name and the app link.
5. To remove a playlist from every app, open it here and click Delete.
